Privacy Policy — Pixel War TD

Last updated: October 31, 2026 · Türkçe

This Privacy Policy describes how Enginar Yazılım ("we", "us") handles information when you play Pixel War TD (the "App") on Android.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and never asks for your name, e-mail address or any other directly identifying information. The following data is collected automatically by the App and by the third-party services it uses:

  • Device and advertising identifiers: the Google Advertising ID and app instance identifiers.
  • Advertising data: the ads shown to you and your interactions with them (impressions, clicks, rewarded-ad completions) and the revenue reported for them.
  • Usage and gameplay data: sessions, level starts, wins and losses, tutorial progress, in-game currency earned and spent, and approximate device information (model, OS version, language, country derived from the IP address).
  • Purchase information: which in-app product was bought, its price and currency. Payments are processed by Google Play; we never receive your card or billing details.
  • Diagnostics: crash and error reports.

Your game progress (levels, coins, boosters, settings) is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

2. How We Use This Information

  • To show advertising, which keeps the App free (interstitial and rewarded ads).
  • To process and deliver in-app purchases.
  • To measure our advertising campaigns (install attribution).
  • To understand how the game is played, balance its difficulty and improve it (analytics, remote configuration).
  • To find and fix crashes and errors.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses these services, which may collect the data above under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (advertising and consent) — Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses data from partner apps
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config — Firebase Privacy and Security
  • GameAnalytics (game analytics) — GameAnalytics Privacy Policy
  • Meta (Facebook SDK: install attribution and app events for advertising) — Meta Privacy Policy

4. Consent and Your Choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any data is collected for ads or analytics, a consent form (Google UMP) asks for your choice. You can decline and still play with non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings › Privacy.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Android Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Purchases: you can manage them in your Google Play account.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Retention and Security

Identifiers, analytics and diagnostics data are kept by the services listed above under their own retention policies. We do not run our own servers or keep our own database of user data. Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

7. Your Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK, CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Because the App has no account, include the details of your request (for example your device's Advertising ID) when you contact us. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. We do not sell your personal information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
